## Repository Overview

This repository contains a comprehensive academic work on medical aesthetics psychology titled "觉察之美：身体美学与医美决策心理学" (The Awareness of Beauty: Body Aesthetics and Medical Aesthetics Decision Psychology). The content is primarily in Chinese and represents an interdisciplinary study combining psychology, philosophy, sociology, and medical aesthetics.

## Repository Structure

The repository consists of:
- `contents.md` - Detailed table of contents with chapter outlines and structure
- `scripts.md` - Complete manuscript content for the book

## Content Architecture

The work is organized into seven main sections:

1. **美的根基** (Foundation of Beauty) - Philosophical and scientific foundations
2. **社会镜像** (Social Mirror) - Cultural and digital influences on beauty standards
3. **内在世界** (Inner World) - Personality, body image, and psychological health
4. **决策黑箱** (Decision Black Box) - Cognitive biases and rational choice
5. **信任的桥梁** (Bridge of Trust) - Doctor-patient relationships and communication
6. **失序的追求** (Disordered Pursuit) - Addiction, trauma, and crisis intervention
7. **未来的图景** (Future Landscape) - Technology, ethics, and evolution of beauty

## Key Theoretical Framework

The work centers around Richard Shusterman's "Somaesthetics" (Body Aesthetics) with three dimensions:
- **Object Body Consciousness** - Analytical examination of the body
- **Embodied Consciousness** - Internal body awareness and sensation
- **Environmental Interactive Consciousness** - Social presentation and interaction

## Target Audience

The content is designed for three primary audiences:
1. **Consumers** - Tools for aesthetic awareness and decision-making
2. **Practitioners** - Medical professionals, consultants, and managers
3. **Researchers** - Students and scholars in psychology and medical aesthetics
